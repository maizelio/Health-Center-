Community Health Center is a static, multi-page website (HTML and CSS only) for a community clinic, with the tagline “Caring for Your Health.”

Pages

Landing (healthcenter.html): welcome text with Login and Sign Up buttons
Login and Sign Up: account forms, with Google and Facebook buttons on sign-up
Home: clinic hours, five services (General Checkup, Vaccination, Maternal Care, Dental Care, Emergency Treatment), mission, vision, and about
Doctors: the general physician, the dentist, and the patient care team
Appointment: a booking form with name, email, service checkboxes, and a message
Supply: an A-Z list of medical supplies with quantities
Design: a teal theme with a shared header, nav, and footer, all styled from one styles.css, and responsive for tablets and phones.

To run it: keep all the files in one folder and open healthcenter.html in a browser.
